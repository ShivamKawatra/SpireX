A few quick answers

Before you reach out.

Here are answers to a few questions we often hear from new members of the SpireX community.

Who can join SpireX Foundation?

Anyone motivated to learn practical skills is welcome, from complete beginners to early-career professionals looking to grow.

Do I need experience before I start?

No. Several programs and internship tracks are designed for beginners, with mentors to help you progress step by step.

How can I ask about an internship?

Choose the internship topic in the form and tell us which area interests you. We will help direct your question.

Can I get involved as a mentor or partner?

Yes. Choose the mentoring or partnership topic and share a little about how you would like to contribute.

Not sure where to begin?

Send us a note and we can help you find a good starting point.
